Frequently asked questions

What is an Enterprise Visitor Management System (VMS)?

A platform that manages and tracks everyone entering and leaving your premises, covering check-in, security and a complete visit record for compliance.

How does an Enterprise VMS differ from a standard VMS?

It adds integrations with other security systems, scale, multi-location support and deeper analytics. Standard tools are more basic.

Why do large organisations need one?

It keeps security high, copes with large visitor volumes, supports compliance and shows visitor patterns.

Can small businesses benefit?

Yes, especially if they are growing or handle sensitive information.

How does it improve security?

It monitors access, links with other security systems, keeps visitor logs and limits areas to authorised people.

Is it scalable?

Yes. It grows with your size and complexity.

Which industries benefit most?

Technology, healthcare, education, government and corporates, especially those with sensitive data or many visitors.

Is it easy for visitors and employees to use?

Yes. Visitors get simple screens and employees get easy management tools.

Can it be customised?

Yes. You can align it with your processes, branding and security rules.

What are the key components?

Check-in and check-out, ID verification, badge printing, security integrations, analytics and compliance tools.
