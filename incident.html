Incident Management

  • Instant incident reporting
  • Escalation and alerts

Turn incidents into learning, not paperwork

Give everyone a simple way to report what went wrong, and give your team the tools to respond, investigate and prevent it from happening again.

Why teams choose it

Report in seconds

Staff and guards log an incident from a phone or kiosk, with photos, location and a short description.

The right people alerted

Email and SMS alerts reach responders based on the type and severity of the incident.

Clear ownership

Assign every incident, set due dates and see its status at any moment.

Automatic escalation

If an incident is not acknowledged or resolved in time, it moves up to the next level.

Investigation and actions

Record the root cause and the corrective and preventive actions taken.

Reports and trends

See incident types, locations and response times, and act before problems repeat.

How it works

Report

Someone logs the incident with details, photos and location.

Alert and assign

Responders are notified and an owner is assigned.

Investigate and resolve

The team records findings and completes the actions.

Review and prevent

Managers study trends and close the loop.

Frequently asked questions

What is incident management software?

It lets you record incidents, alert the right people, assign and track the response, and keep a full history for reviews and audits.

Who can report an incident?

Anyone you allow, such as employees, guards and front desk staff, from a phone or a kiosk.

Can I set different severity levels?

Yes. Classify incidents by type and priority, and set the alerts and deadlines for each.

What happens if nobody responds in time?

Escalation rules move the incident to the next level so it does not stay unattended.

Can I attach photos and documents?

Yes. Evidence, statements and files can be attached to each incident.

Does it help with audits?

Yes. Every update is time-stamped, and reports can be exported for reviews and audits.
