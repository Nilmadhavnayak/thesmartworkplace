Frequently asked questions

Can I monitor all my delivery vehicles from one place?

Yes. One dashboard covers delivery orders, vehicle movement and driver performance, for one location or many.

How does it help save costs?

Better routes, less idle time and fewer delivery errors cut fuel, labour waste and delays.

How does it improve driver and vehicle efficiency?

It records reporting times, entry and exit logs and live tracking, so managers can allocate vehicles better and avoid delays.
