Frequently asked questions

What are the biggest problems with manual outpass management?

It is slow, error-prone and opaque. Employees chase approvals, security struggles to track movement, and HR has no central view.

How does digital outpass improve approvals?

Employees request movement digitally, and department heads and HR approve by email with automatic notifications.

Can security see movements in real time?

Yes. A consolidated dashboard shows approved movements instantly.

How does it improve compliance and safety?

Central movement records mean only approved employees leave, with detailed reports for audits.

Will it reduce approval delays?

Yes. Requests take minutes to raise, and approvers are notified instantly by email.

Can we generate reports for audits?

Yes. Every movement is logged, and central MIS reports can be downloaded for audits and analysis.
